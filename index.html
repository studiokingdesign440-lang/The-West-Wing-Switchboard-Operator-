THE SWITCHBOARDOPERATOR

You are the new Director of the Information Office.
You have a desk, a headset, a board of twelve lines,
and eleven people who already know more than you do.

Every call reaches you first. Patch it correctly.
Wrong desk, and the building eats you alive.

Seven days. Two shifts. A great many telephones.
